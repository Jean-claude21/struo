# Landing (/) — surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owner of a construction company (5–200 people). Action: open the role demo (primary), start the 14-day trial (secondary). Proof: the product at work (live "Today" console, offline report simulation), sourced industry figures, the visitor's own estimate. Constraints: international (no country names), prices in the visitor's country currency or "on quote", no guarantee, no invented testimonial.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Good management made visible. The page shows a company's day arriving as signals and turning into calm decisions; it refuses the split hero over a row of equal feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: site-ink green-black ground for hero and decision bands, paper for reading bands, one orange (#ec5426 family) for action and emphasis words; DM Sans heavy and tight for headlines, DM Mono only for times, site codes and amounts; hairline rules, flat panels, real site photos.
STORY: you do good work, it stays invisible and costs time (sourced), Struo shows what, when, where, each role its part, the client sees a professional company, priced for your country, open the demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: dark band; left the headline "Ce qui est bien géré se voit." at display scale with the orange emphasis, lead, primary demo button and trial link; right the live Today console (map with five sites, the day's signals cycling); below the fold edge an orange ribbon of the day's events scrolling.
