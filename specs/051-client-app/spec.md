# 051 — The client's application

**Branch**: `051-client-app` (base `050-client-report-v2`) · **Status**: delivered for review
**Answers** (`docs/product/roles.md`): client « what is new? », « what waits for me? », « where is the
money, how do I pay? », « can I ask about what I see? », « am I told without opening the space? »,
« can I choose what I receive? »; accountant « which clients say they paid? ».

## The space, four tabs

- **Home**: « New since your visit on … » (reports, photos, messages, change orders, invoices,
  documents — what the link lets them see), **To do** (change orders to answer, stages to sign off,
  invoices due within 3 days and not already reported paid; « Nothing is waiting for you »), the
  progress with the next step, the latest report's summary, the photos.
- **Reports**: the reports the company sent; the shared photos arranged by stage. Raw field reports
  only when the company chose so for that client (off by default).
- **Invoices** (and documents): where the money stands (original contract, accepted change orders,
  total, invoiced, paid, left to pay, left to invoice), invoices to pay, **How to pay** (the company's
  own words from its invoice settings: bank transfer, mobile payment, terms — Struo never holds the
  money), **« I paid »** with an optional proof, decided change orders, history, shared documents.
- **Messages**: as before; « Ask a question » from a field report opens it with the context written.
- The client chooses whether they receive news by e-mail (access codes always go).
- A visit within 30 minutes of the previous one keeps the same « since ».

## « I paid »

- The client declares an amount, a date, a method, a reference and an optional photo/PDF proof
  (`/api/portal/<token>/claim`). One pending declaration per invoice.
- The owner, admins and accountants are e-mailed and notified; the queue shows on the accountant's
  and the owner's home and in Finance › Declared payments. **Confirm** records the payment on the
  invoice; **Refuse** keeps a reason the client reads. Nothing counts as paid before a confirmation.
- A company can turn « I paid » off (Settings › Invoicing).

## Phone notifications (Web Push)

- The space installs like an app (its own manifest, named after the site). From the second visit it
  offers to turn notifications on; on iPhone it explains « Add to Home Screen » first.
- Sent to the client's phones: report sent, change order to answer, stage to sign off, new invoice,
  reply from the office, payment confirmed or refused. E-mail as well for the invoice, the stage and
  a refused payment (the report and change order have their own e-mails) — unless the client opted
  out. Never from a demo company.
- One VAPID key pair per environment, generated by the deploy script; without keys, nothing is offered.
- Team members' phones use the same table (spec 052).
