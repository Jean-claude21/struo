# 024 — Team

**Branch**: `020-design-system` · **Status**: delivered

`/app/team`: members with role and last activity, role change and removal, pending invitations (copy, revoke), and an invitation that starts from the role (illustrated cards, one line each) then the channel (WhatsApp, e-mail, link). A person may belong to several companies with a role in each; the switcher sits at the top of the sidebar.
