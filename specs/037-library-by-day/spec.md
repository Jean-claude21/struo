# 037 — The library by day

**Branch**: `039-library-by-day` · **Status**: delivered

Documents › Library gets a third view beside the grid and the list: **by day**. Files (photos,
voice notes, documents, invoices) are grouped by the day they were taken or made, newest day first,
each day with its count, its sites and who made them, a header that stays visible while scrolling,
and « Aller au » to jump to a day. The type, site and search filters apply as in the other views.
